# Open questions for Kazakhstan counsel (not legal advice, not answered)

Written so a lawyer can review quickly. Nothing here is a conclusion.

1. Personal data: which fields on a company report count as personal data (directors, beneficial
   owners, public officials)? What lawful basis covers displaying them, and does the answer differ
   for officials in their official capacity?
2. Cross-border: reports are sold to foreign buyers. Are there transfer, localisation or storage
   requirements for the data we hold?
3. Scraping: for each portal, do the terms or national rules restrict automated access or
   redistribution? Are open-data licences compatible with a paid product?
4. Historical snapshots: may we keep and show records the source has since removed or corrected?
   What takedown / correction process is expected?
5. Defamation: how should risk-flag wording be reviewed so an indicator is not read as an accusation?
6. Sanctions/PEP data: licensing terms for a commercial report product (see OpenSanctions licence).
7. Do we need registration or notification as a data operator?

## Added 2026-10-13 for the concierge service (`launch/`)
8. Where the work is done: client briefs, counterparty extracts (directors' names, possibly IINs) and unencrypted
   backups sit on the operator's device, possibly outside Kazakhstan. Do localisation and consent rules apply to that,
   beyond question 2?
9. Paid briefs: may results of manual lookups on stat.gov.kz, kgd.gov.kz or egov, or material from aggregator
   subscriptions (Adata, Kompra), be quoted in a paid client deliverable?
10. Client and counterparty documents: what confidentiality terms, retention and deletion period and permitted use
    apply? Is an NDA or engagement letter needed?
11. Selling the service: registration, tax invoicing and VAT for cross-border USD fees; what the payment provider's KYC
    will require.
12. Liability: engagement terms and limitation of liability for a paid brief; how to word a "discrepancy" so it is not
    read as an allegation (extends question 5 from flags to briefs).
13. Licensing: does commercial due-diligence or information-analytical work on Kazakh companies need a licence (for
    example under private-detective rules)?
14. Cold outreach: commercial-message rules in recipients' countries (Turkey, Italy/EU, Latvia, China, Kazakhstan), and
    whether storing prospects' business contacts is personal-data processing.
15. Brand: is "Ashyq Dala" clear to use as a trade name?
