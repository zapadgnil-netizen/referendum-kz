# Product

## Register

product

## Users
One independent analyst (the operator) running a hand-delivered research service on Kazakh companies. They work
alone, in long sessions, switching between their sales pipeline, a research case's evidence ledger and the client
brief they are about to send. Secondary audience: the buyer (an equipment exporter, supplier or research firm) who
sees only the exported public offer page and the delivered brief.

## Product Purpose
Ashyq Dala's Partner Desk lets one person sell and deliver a sourced, one-company research brief: track leads and
cash, log every source with its date and attribution, and export a brief that separates evidence from uncertainty.
Success is a first paid brief delivered on time with no unsourced claim in it.

## Brand Personality
Sharp, investigative, fast. The voice of a careful investigator: specific nouns, dated facts, no hype. The interface
should feel like a working instrument, dense where the data is dense, and quick to move through.

## Anti-references
- Spy-movie theatrics: fake radar sweeps, "CLASSIFIED" stamps, rotating fake progress text, decorative scanlines.
- Anything that implies certainty the evidence does not give (risk gauges, red/green verdicts on a company).

## Design Principles
1. Evidence before conclusion: sources, dates and attribution status are always visible next to the claim.
2. Uncertainty is shown, not hidden: unresolved and name-only matches look different from confirmed ones.
3. Speed over ceremony: few clicks, no decorative motion, dense tables where they help.
4. Private and public are visibly different: the desk is a workspace; anything that leaves it is labelled.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG 2.2 AA: 4.5:1 body contrast on the dark surface, visible focus rings, full keyboard use, reduced-motion
respected. Status is never carried by color alone (badges have text). Cyrillic and Latin text must both render well.
