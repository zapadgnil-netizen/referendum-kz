## Verification limits

Full browser interaction and visual checks could not be completed in this environment: no local Chromium binary was available, allowed installation attempts were blocked, and the cloud browser could not open the local app. The Node tests use a minimal DOM harness and do not prove rendering, mobile layout, native form behavior, mail-app integration, downloads, or printing in a real browser. Test the receiving-email flow and one harmless sample export in your normal browser before sharing the offer.

The app has no server, hosted URL, automatic data collection, authenticated multi-user access, live payment processing, or verified commercial source feed. Local browser storage and downloaded backups are unencrypted. The customer form opens a draft for the customer to send, or prepares a downloadable request; it does not submit to a backend.

No customer has been contacted and no charge has been made. The original uploaded demo and handoff were left intact. The rebuilt product focuses on selling and delivering a bounded manual research service.
