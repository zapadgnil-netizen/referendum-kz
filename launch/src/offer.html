Clear expectations

A defined brief.
Honest boundaries.

The scope is based on accessible evidence. You will see what was checked, what was found and what remains unknown.

Which information can you verify?

The available sources and supplied documents are reviewed before the engagement is accepted. The brief may cover identity details and relevant business records where access and permitted use are confirmed. Ownership, court, property and financial information are only included when suitable evidence is available and agreed in scope.

Is this a compliance clearance or credit rating?

No. This is commercial research to support your own review. It does not certify that a business is safe, approve a transaction, provide legal advice or replace specialist sanctions, financial or regulatory due diligence.

What if the sources are incomplete?

Unavailable records and unresolved matches are explicitly listed. If the initial access review shows that the proposed work cannot be useful, the scope is adjusted before payment or the engagement is declined. A missing record is never presented as a clean result.

How is the two-day delivery window calculated?

The target is two business days after scope, source access, the agreed documents and the deposit are confirmed. Your deadline is checked before acceptance. Delays requiring additional counterparty documents are discussed with you.

How do I share sensitive documents?

The first request only needs a company name and a short description of your goal. An appropriate document-sharing method is agreed directly before you send materials. This page does not upload documents or store form details.

Can you research a person or an individual entrepreneur?

No. The service covers legal entities identified by a BIN. Individual entrepreneurs are identified by a personal IIN, so researching one would mean profiling a private person, which is outside scope. Directors and owners appear only as they are named in company records relevant to the agreed question.

Can I research several companies?

Yes, you can describe the broader need in your request. The pilot covers one company. Additional entities or ongoing work require a separate agreed scope and quote.
